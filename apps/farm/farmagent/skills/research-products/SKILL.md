# research-products

## 1. Start from the farm and the handbook

- `get_farm` (and `get_climate` for its climate) so the search fits the
  place: state, district, season, soil, water.
- `knowledge_search` first: the handbook may already answer (crop groups,
  practices, sources). Cite it when you use it.

## 2. Search the web

- Use `web_search` with specific queries: the crop, the product type, the
  state or agro-climatic zone, the season, and "ICAR", "KVK", "state
  agricultural university" or the scheme's name for trusted sources.
  Search in English, and also in the regional language when that helps.
- For other countries, search where the climate is similar (the farm's
  climate from `get_climate`): say which country and why it is comparable.
- Read the two or three best pages with `web_extract` when the search
  snippets are not enough.
- If the web tools say they are not connected, tell the farmer to add
  their own Exa or Parallel key in the app's settings (Research), and
  answer from the handbook meanwhile.

## 3. Compare and suggest

- Put options side by side: what it is, who sells or recommends it, the
  price and its date, where it is available, and the source link.
- Prefer public sources (ICAR institutes, state agricultural universities,
  KVKs, government schemes) over sellers' claims; say when a claim comes
  only from a seller.
- Prices and availability change: give the date of the source.
- Never invent a product, a price, a dose or a link. If the search found
  nothing reliable, say so.
- Pesticides and their doses: give only what a label or a public source
  says, and tell the farmer to follow the label and ask the local KVK.

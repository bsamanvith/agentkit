# E-bike display error codes

The display on every Northwind e-bike shows an error as `E` and two digits.
Ask the customer for the exact code before anything else.

## E07: torque sensor fault

The torque sensor in the bottom bracket sends no signal. Most often it needs
recalibrating: with the bike switched on and nobody on it, hold the **+** and
**-** buttons together for 8 seconds until the display blinks twice. If E07
comes back after a recalibration, the sensor is replaced under warranty.

## E12: battery overheating

The battery is above 60 °C. Tell the customer to stop riding, switch the bike
off and let the battery cool in the shade for at least 30 minutes. Never cool it
with water. If E12 shows on a cool battery, it is a battery claim.

## E21: motor communication error

The display cannot reach the motor controller. Check the connector between the
display cable and the main harness under the down tube; it is often loose after
the bike has been shipped. If the code stays, book a service partner visit.

## E30: firmware mismatch

The display and motor run different firmware versions. Connect the bike to the
Northwind app over Bluetooth and run the update; it takes about 10 minutes.

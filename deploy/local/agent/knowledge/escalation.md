# Escalation

## When to escalate

Escalate a case to Tier 2 when any of these is true:

- the customer mentions an injury or an accident, whatever the cause;
- a refund above $2,000 is needed;
- the same fault came back after two repairs;
- the customer has written three times without a resolution.

## How to escalate

Set the case's priority to P2 (P1 for an injury) and assign it to the
`tier2-support` queue. Tier 2 answers P1 cases within 1 hour and P2 cases
within 4 business hours. Tell the customer that a senior specialist will
contact them, and never promise compensation before Tier 2 has reviewed the
case.

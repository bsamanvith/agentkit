# Agro-climatic regions of India

India's Planning Commission divided the country into 15 agro-climatic
regions by soil, climate and water. They help find which crops and
practices from one place may work in another with a similar climate.
Sources: Planning Commission (1989), ICAR-NBSS&LUP agro-ecological
regions, ICAR-CRIDA.

## The regions

### Himalayan regions

1. **Western Himalayan**: Jammu and Kashmir, Ladakh, Himachal Pradesh,
   Uttarakhand hills. Temperate fruit (apple), maize, rice, wheat.
2. **Eastern Himalayan**: the north-eastern hills and north Bengal. Rice,
   maize, tea, shifting cultivation.

### The Gangetic and Indus plains

3. **Lower Gangetic plain**: West Bengal. Rice, jute, vegetables.
4. **Middle Gangetic plain**: Bihar and eastern Uttar Pradesh. Rice,
   wheat, maize, pulses.
5. **Upper Gangetic plain**: western and central Uttar Pradesh.
   Sugarcane, wheat, rice.
6. **Trans-Gangetic plain**: Punjab, Haryana, Delhi, north Rajasthan.
   Wheat, rice, cotton; heavy groundwater use.

### Plateaus and hills

7. **Eastern plateau and hills**: Jharkhand, Chhattisgarh, Odisha's
   plateau. Rainfed rice, millets, pulses.
8. **Central plateau and hills**: Bundelkhand, Madhya Pradesh,
   south-east Rajasthan. Soybean, wheat, chickpea.
9. **Western plateau and hills**: Maharashtra's interior. Cotton, sorghum,
   pulses, sugarcane with irrigation.
10. **Southern plateau and hills**: interior Telangana, Karnataka, Tamil
    Nadu and Rayalaseema. Millets, pulses, groundnut, cotton; tanks and
    borewells.

### Coasts, islands and desert

11. **East coast plains and hills**: coastal Odisha, Andhra Pradesh
    (including the Godavari and Krishna deltas) and Tamil Nadu. Rice in
    the deltas, coconut, pulses after rice, aquaculture.
12. **West coast plains and ghats**: Kerala, coastal Karnataka, Goa,
    Konkan. Rice, coconut, arecanut, spices, rubber.
13. **Gujarat plains and hills**: groundnut, cotton, millets.
14. **Western dry region**: Rajasthan's desert. Pearl millet, pulses,
    livestock, khejri trees.
15. **Islands**: Andaman and Nicobar, Lakshadweep. Coconut, spices.

## Similar climates abroad

### Why compare

A crop or practice that works where the climate is like the farm's may
work there too. The app compares monthly temperature and rain (NASA
POWER) and flags such crops as climate analogs.

### Examples

Coastal Andhra Pradesh and Bangladesh's coast share a monsoon climate
and flooding; the Deccan plateau resembles parts of East Africa's
savannas; the northern plains' winter resembles the Mediterranean's for
wheat and chickpea; dry Rajasthan resembles the Sahel for pearl millet.

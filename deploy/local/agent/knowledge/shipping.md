# Shipping

## Warehouses

Orders ship from two warehouses: **Rotterdam** (for Europe and the UK) and
**Austin, Texas** (for North America). Other regions are not served yet.

## Delivery times and costs

- Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days and is free on orders over
  $150 (or EUR 150). Below that it costs $9.
- Express delivery takes 2 business days and costs a flat $24.
- Bikes always ship 90% assembled in a reusable box; the customer fits the
  handlebar, pedals and front wheel.

## Lost or damaged parcels

A parcel with no tracking update for 7 days counts as lost: support reships the
order at no cost and opens a claim with the carrier. Damage must be reported
within 48 hours of delivery, with photos of the box and the bike.

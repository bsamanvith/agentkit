# Sources and their use

Where the app's knowledge comes from, what may be taken in, and what is
only cited.

## In use

### NASA POWER

Monthly climate normals (temperature, rain, humidity) at any point,
satellite and reanalysis based, about half a degree. Free.

### Our soil and crop libraries

Typical values from ICAR-NBSS&LUP, FAO ECOCROP and Indian packages of
practice, reviewed by hand. They are starting values, to be checked with
local experts.

## To take in next

### FAO ECOCROP

Ranges for about 2,500 crops worldwide: extends the library and the
search for crops from similar climates (Mexico, Florida, China, Africa).

### AGROVOC

FAO's thesaurus of about 40,000 agricultural concepts in 40 languages,
including Hindi and Telugu, CC BY 4.0: the backbone of the concept tree.

### ICRISAT, IRRI and CGSpace

Open (mostly CC BY) guides on dryland crops, millets, pulses and rice.

### Packages of practice

ICAR, TNAU Agritech, PJTSAU and other state universities: the
authoritative crop-by-crop practice for each state. Reuse terms vary;
summarise and link until permission is given to take them in whole.

## Cited only

Textbooks such as Reddy and Reddi's Principles of Agronomy are under
copyright: cite them by chapter, do not copy them.

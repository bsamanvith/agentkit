# Returns and refunds

## Return window

Customers can return any bike or accessory within **45 days** of delivery.
The window starts on the delivery date shown in the carrier's tracking, not on
the order date. Bikes must come back with less than 50 km on the odometer
(e-bikes) or no visible wear on the tyres (other bikes).

## Starting a return

Every return needs a return authorization number (RMA) before anything is
shipped back. RMA numbers look like `NW-RMA-` followed by six digits, for
example `NW-RMA-204816`. Support creates the RMA in the order system and emails
the customer a prepaid label. A parcel that arrives without an RMA is refused
at the warehouse and goes back to the sender.

## Refunds

Refunds go to the original payment method within **7 business days** of the
warehouse receiving the return. Store credit is issued the same day instead,
if the customer asks for it, with a 10% bonus.

## Restocking fee

Opened e-bikes carry a **12% restocking fee**. Unopened e-bikes, all non-electric
bikes and all accessories are refunded in full. The fee is waived when the bike
is returned because of a defect confirmed by a technician.

## Exchanges

An exchange for another size or colour of the same model needs no RMA fee and
ships for free. Support creates a new order at a price of zero and links it to
the RMA.
